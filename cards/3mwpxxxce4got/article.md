The [Workflows & Process Integration charter](https://github.com/aaif/wg-workflows-and-process-integration/blob/main/charter/charter.md) explicitly covers interrupted workflows, state persistence, retries, idempotency, external-system coordination, and portability across runtimes. This makes it a direct place to discuss dependable ongoing agent execution.

Participation is open to individuals and organizations. The joining steps are to join the mailing list, complete CLA/DCO requirements if applicable, and request a working-group invitation. Formal voting rights are separate; the charter reserves some votes to Platinum and Gold members.

Start from the [official repository](https://github.com/aaif/wg-workflows-and-process-integration), which links the public mailing list, Discord, meeting access, and notes. A Linux Foundation account provides access to the LFX meeting dashboard and recordings. Check the [public calendar](https://aaif.io/working-groups/calendar) for current calls.

A proposed first contribution: document one interrupted-agent scenario, the evidence needed to know whether its external action happened, and the recovery behavior required across runtimes. Discuss the scope before building a large implementation.
