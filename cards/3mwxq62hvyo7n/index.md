Basin is Cloudflare's serverless analytics stack around R2 and Apache Iceberg: Pipelines ingests and transforms events, Catalog manages the Iceberg tables, and Basin SQL queries them. Its purpose is to make an open lakehouse-style pipeline operationally simple.
