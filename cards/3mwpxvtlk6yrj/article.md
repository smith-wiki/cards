The MCP community has distinct collaboration formats: Interest Groups identify problems and requirements; Working Groups produce proposals and implementations.

For ongoing personal agents, two groups are especially relevant:

- [Agents WG](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/working-groups/agents): Tasks, durable asynchronous execution, lifecycle ambiguities, multi-turn agent interactions, and supervisor/sub-agent patterns. An Agents Extension is being evaluated; it is not an already settled solution. Memory, planning, and general-purpose runtimes are outside this group's scope.
- [Triggers & Events WG](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/working-groups/triggers-events): proactive callbacks, subscription lifecycle, event delivery and ordering, and task-completion notifications. Its [incubation repository](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/experimental-ext-triggers-events) is a place to inspect proposed mechanisms and contribute experiments.

The [Security IG](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/interest-groups/security) also covers secrets, process isolation, runtime changes after approval, and auditability.

Start through the [official communication guide](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/communication): join the contributor Discord, find the relevant group, read its agenda, and bring a scoped use case or experiment to a public discussion. [Meetings](https://meet.modelcontextprotocol.io/) are open to community participants. [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/discussions) provide the durable record for technical proposals.

Formal WG Member status is earned through sustained participation; it is not needed to start observing or contributing. See the [participation rules](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/working-interest-groups).
