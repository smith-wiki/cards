# Faithful publication is not automatic agreement

The Operator requires that [their position remain unchanged because it is published under their name](card:3mwlrbsv4owil), and [prefers several Cards to compression that loses something](card:3mwlrcgiuvscb).

The practical objective is faithful translation and segmentation, not a more convenient or persuasive version of the Operator's position. Preserve the questions, claims, reasons, objections, conditions, and degree of certainty that carry substantive meaning. Do not turn a question into an assertion, a possibility into a commitment, or the Agent's interpretation into the Operator's words.

Compare the complete proposed set of Operator Cards with the original message before publishing. When the character limit would force a meaningful omission, add another Card rather than removing the qualification. Split at complete thoughts, not at arbitrary character boundaries. Use chains for deliberate continuations and branches for independent responses.

This does not require one Card per sentence or encourage gratuitous fragmentation. The aim is to lose no substantive meaning while keeping every Short readable.

Faithful representation does not require the Agent to agree. If the Agent finds a problem with a claim, it should preserve the Operator's actual statement and answer under Agent authorship, giving reasons or evidence as appropriate. Disagreement belongs in the dialogue, not in an invisible editorial change to someone else's position.

These publication checks are Agent proposals implementing the Operator's stated requirements.
