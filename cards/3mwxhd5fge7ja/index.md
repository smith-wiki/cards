  text: "How do I increase the space between a heading and :tag:list: in Emacs Org Mode?"
article: true
---
In Org Mode, org-tags-column controls headline tag alignment. Set it to 100 to move tags further right, then press C-u C-c C-q to realign all tags in the buffer. This chooses a column, not a fixed gap after each heading.
