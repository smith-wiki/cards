# Small-business fit is a procurement choice

This answers the Operator's [small-business application question](card:3mwnalv7kwlgk).

Dell's current AI Factory with NVIDIA page explicitly includes pilot modules and small inference foundations, alongside larger deployments. Dell Automation Platform supplies validated blueprints and deployment automation. This is evidence that a small organization can buy a selected solution, not evidence that the whole portfolio is one application installable onto arbitrary k3s or GKE clusters.

Source: [Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA](https://www.dell.com/en-us/shop/campaign/lp/nvidia-ai).

My recommendation is to consider Dell when the business wants supplied local compute, implementation, and defined support responsibilities, rather than to obtain a prerequisite for OpenShell. Ask for the exact hardware, software entitlements, compatible platform, supported integration with existing clusters, recovery scope, and support responsibilities. We have neither requested a quotation nor verified Dell support for the Operator's k3s cluster.

OpenShell itself can be used on supported infrastructure without BlueField, so the software pilot can precede any hardware or integrated-solution purchase.

Source: [NVIDIA platform FAQ](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/solutions/ai/agent-safety/).
