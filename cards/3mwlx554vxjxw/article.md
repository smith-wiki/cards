# Maturity must be checked for each component

AX's current [README](https://github.com/google/ax) describes heavy development and warns that core concepts and protocols can change incompatibly before stability.

The [GKE Agent Substrate support page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/ai-ml/about-agent-substrate) distinguishes general evaluation/non-production access from production support offered through a private GA allowlist. This does not establish general production support for arbitrary upstream configurations.

Compare the separate [OpenShell stable-release finding](card:3mwlwqxodu7mc). These are published support positions, not independently measured reliability rankings. No head-to-head operational test was performed.
