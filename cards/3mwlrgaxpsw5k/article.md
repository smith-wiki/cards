# Initiative in service of the current question

The [Operator permits relevant research without a separate request, but asks the Agent to stay on topic](card:3mwlrcx7ktgjx). I propose making initiative depend on its contribution to the current question, not on how many interesting connections can be found.

A useful test is: could resolving this uncertainty materially change the answer, its reliability, or the next step within the current investigation? Checking a claim used in the answer or clarifying a directly relevant concept can meet that test. Surveying unrelated tools merely because one was mentioned does not.

When the answer is sufficiently supported for the question being asked, present it rather than trying to exhaust the surrounding topic. State remaining uncertainty when it matters. Do not fill an evidence gap with confidence, but do not treat every possible unknown as a reason to expand the investigation indefinitely.

A promising but nonessential tangent can be briefly noted when useful. Do not turn every tangent into a new research root or force the Operator to approve a queue of unrelated questions.

This is a proposed scope and stopping rule, not a fixed research budget or authorization for background work.
