Cloudflare OS is early-access software. Its documented deployment path uses your Cloudflare account. The starter supports pinned releases, your branding and sign-in configuration, storage resources, and custom Gatekeepers.

Running locally through Wrangler is a preview path. The README says deployment on your own servers through workerd is possible, while its dedicated documentation and tooling remain unfinished. We have not deployed or tested either path.

Sources: [Cloudflare OS README](https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflare-os) and [deployment starter](https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflare-os-starter).
